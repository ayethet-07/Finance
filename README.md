# OpsFinance Studio 📊

> **Digital Operational Governance & Fiscal Simulation Suites for Independent Professionals & Micro-Agencies in Spain.**

OpsFinance Studio is an operational e-commerce platform developed by **Aye Thet Thet Khaing** (MSc in Project Management). It bridges the gap between project management theory (PMBOK® / Earned Value Management) and local fiscal compliance in Spain (RETA progressive brackets, IRPF Modelos 130/100, and IVA Modelo 303).

## ✨ Features & Capabilities

- **Interactive Spanish Fiscal Simulator:** 
  - Dynamic monthly RETA quota calculation (Year 1 Tarifa Plana vs. 15-bracket progressive system).
  - 7% statutory deduction for difficult-to-justify expenses (*Gastos de Difícil Justificación*, capped at €2,000).
  - Progressive personal income tax (*IRPF Escala General*) net retention modeling.
  - Recommended minimum billable hourly and daily rate engine.
- **Product Architecture:**
  - **Tier 1 — Essential Freelance Calculator (€29):** True billable rate calculation, capacity planning, and base overhead recovery.
  - **Tier 2 — Full Operating & Fiscal Suite (€59):** Complete operational hub with quarterly cash-flow forecasting (Modelos 303 & 130) and Scope Creep EVM loggers.
  - **Tier 3 — Agency B2B Implementation Pack (€149):** Multi-seat team license + 45-minute asynchronous operational onboarding audit.
- **EU & Spanish Compliance Ready:**
  - Native support for standard Spanish payment flows (**Bizum**, Stripe, PayPal).
  - Explicit EU digital consumer rights waiver notice at checkout.
  - Built-in GDPR (*RGPD*) privacy and data protection framework.
